What we do

A digital marketing agency
built for purpose-led brands.

We help sustainability-driven brands, NGOs, and impact entrepreneurs grow their reach through structured strategy and digital systems. Unlike traditional agencies, we offer personal, story-first support: strategic storytelling, content creation and visibility coaching in one coherent service.

We exist to give the new generation of changemakers the authentic voice they need to lead, inspire, and grow sustainably. No excuses. No extractive tactics. Just honest digital growth.

The founder

Lise Kriekemans.

Lise built Wilma Collective around everything she cares about: languages, storytelling, strategy and social change. With a background in Business Communication and Cultural Anthropology, and years working with NGOs including Trias, Caritas, Studio Globo and Studio Zambia, she brings a perspective few marketers have.

Working across Europe and Africa gave her a clear view of how communication drives real change and how often it falls short. Wilma Collective exists to close that gap. Purpose-led organisations deserve the same quality of digital thinking as any commercial brand.

Fun fact

Wilma is Lise’s middle name.

Kriekemans means cherries in Dutch, and the name’s origin also nods to her godfather Wim and Wilma Flinstone, a logical, inventive woman known for her resourcefulness.
